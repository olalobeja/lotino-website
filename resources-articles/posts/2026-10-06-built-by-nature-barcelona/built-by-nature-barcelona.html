The Built by Nature Summit 2026 held in Barcelona, the 2026 world architecture capital of the world, with underlying pursuits on how to deliver housing at scale while staying within planetary boundaries. How can we continue Housing the World, without Costing the Earth?

For some reason, albeit severa bio-based materials exist (bamboo, hemp straw) timber tends to keep a central stage on teh bio.based construction conversation always. City Tours centered on timber ends. Perhaps it is because of teh potential for industrialization, scaling, utilization, organized resources that are traceable.

Bamboo grows in weeks, wow! Kudos to those who drive it for construction.

An underlying theme was a need to maintain evidence. keep things within a social economic, environment context. If hybrid with timber gets us far, let us go for it!

Researching structural timber — for Uganda

My backdrop lately remains Uganda. A beautiful Pearl of AFrica, in many ways. The last site visit guided had the lady mention he had visited Uganda and would tell me later during the visit. I did not get to wait for this later, needed to catch the train.

from “forests to frame” is a line I get in the book The TImber Truth. And this to me reminds me as a structural engineer, i am sort of loosely responsibly for teh frame end of it. Is it optimal, safe, against whatever it could be thrown at (including fire)? The forest side we seem to maintain a sufficient FSC supply (Busoga Foresty) that can be enjoyed. Strength grading of course to me is a responsibility of teh saw milling process. You send to market a specific grade, quality. But this does not seem to be happened, maybe becase the market does not demand it.

Code process building code is still a little too simple on this matter in Uganda, in Kenya, in East Africa.

I was surprised at a narrative shared by a specific Spanish architect — that in Spanish legal construction law, design world (architect, engineers) for public procurement are not allowed to interact with the construction side (contractors, builders) before formal contract processes. This is tricky for timber world, because often, all are learning. and if we jump on the drawing board together all of us, it makes life so much easier and more efficient.

Thanks to everyone, and let us continue in our own way building by nature. It looks worthwhile, where it is possible.
